Uptight & Laid-Back: interactive map of the 1960s

Interactive map. Use the year slider to choose a year. Use the arrow keys to pan and the plus and minus keys to zoom. Press Tab to move between buildings, photographs and protests, then Enter or Space to open details, and Escape to close them. A text list of everything shown for the selected year is available from the Text list of features button.
